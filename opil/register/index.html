Supported byThe Interledger Foundation

The Interledger Foundation is a nonprofit working to make digital payments reach everyone — including the people the current financial system leaves out. It stewards the Interledger Protocol, an open standard that lets money move between different payment networks the way email moves between different providers, and funds work that puts open payments to practical use.

It supports the AUC Open Payments Innovation Lab through the grant Building Digital Pathways for Financial Equity, awarded to the Atlanta University Center Consortium Data Science Initiative. Your team’s work in the FinTech track builds directly on that open-payments technology.
