The AI workshop track · 2026–27 program year

The AI Thread.

Fourteen one-hour AI workshops woven through the Innovation Lab year, one for every deliverable the cohort owes next. The curriculum teaches the discipline. The Thread is where the homework gets done, with AI, on free tools, every week.

The fourteen sessions Preview registration
Da'Sci · program host · Data Challenge Finale '26
How it fits

Wednesdays teach.
Mondays, it gets done.

Curriculum sessions run every other Wednesday, with HPC working sessions and office hours between them. The AI Thread takes its own hour on Mondays, so it never competes with a session and never leaves a team stuck between Wednesdays. Each hour points at one deliverable: the one due next.

Every session runs on the free tiers of the major AI tools, live online, and ends with the team’s own result on their own screen.
