What's inside

Everything a cohort needs,
in one place.

Most programs piece this together from five or six different tools. Here it is one sign-in, and the person running the program never needs a developer.

1

A home with your name on it

Your logo beside ours, your colors, and your own address on the site, like taylormadeacademy.com/yourschool. It installs on a phone like an app, straight from the browser. No app store.

2

Sign-up that sorts itself

People apply on your page. Your coordinator approves each one with a tap. Someone joins late? Send a one-time invite link.

3

A live class room

Cameras, chat, polls and screen sharing for everyone. A question line where you bring people “on stage.” Small groups with a timer, a whiteboard, class files and live captions.

4

Every class becomes a replay

The room records itself. Each replay gets chapters, a transcript and the files from class. Your coordinator looks it over, then publishes it.

5

Teams that keep working

Each team gets a page, a chat, a locker for files and checkpoints, and its own room to meet in. Judges score pitches on a leaderboard, and each team can have a public showcase page.

6

One screen for the coordinator

The roster, approvals, sessions, materials and attendance in one place. Calendar invites, a reminder email 30 minutes before class, and a “Need help?” button that reaches your team.

Small groups, from the front of the room. See every group, join any room, message them all, add time.

The coordinator's view. Sessions, files and recordings added without a developer, and attendance in one click. (Sample data.)

For a whole campus

One hub. Every office.

The same platform can carry a whole university. Each office that wants one gets its own space, all behind one sign-in, and it installs on a phone as your campus app.

One place in place of many subscriptions

A lot of campuses pay for an events app, a webinar tool, a community app and a phone app, one bill each. A hub puts them under one roof.

Events app→A campus calendar with add-to-calendar
Webinar tool→The live room, for any office
Community app→Feeds, messages and team spaces
Phone app→Installs from the browser

Beside your LMS, never against it

Your LMS (learning management system) holds grades and credit. The hub sits next to it and does not replace it. It is where people meet, learn live and stay connected.
