The live class room

A class room that opens in the browser.

The real room. Small groups on the clock, the question line open, the recording saving itself.

  • 1Sign in and you're in. Nothing to install, on a laptop or a phone.
  • 2A question line. People raise a hand in order, and you bring them on stage.
  • 3Everyone can share. Polls, chat and screen sharing for the whole room.
  • 4Built-in tools. A whiteboard, class files and live captions.
  • 5A class never ends by accident. Leave only takes you out, End takes two taps, and every class records itself.
Taylormade Academy · The live class room4
Small groups

Small groups, run from the front of the room.

Split the class in two taps. Every group gets its own room, and a clock everyone can see.

From one board you can see who is in each room, drop into any of them, message all of them at once, and add five or ten minutes.

Groups that ask for help show up on the board, so nobody waits in silence.

Between classes

Every class is kept. Every team keeps working.

Replays that do the note-taking

The room records every class on its own. Each replay gets chapters, a transcript and the files from class. Your coordinator looks it over, then publishes it for the cohort.

Teams with a home of their own

  • 1A team page, a chat, and a locker for files and checkpoints.
  • 2A standing room the team can meet in any time.
  • 3Judges score pitches on a leaderboard, and each team can have a public showcase page.

How it fits

Built around how you already work.

Your name on it

A partnership by default: your brand out front, our platform underneath, both names on the door. Carrying your name alone is an option too.

Beside your LMS

Your LMS (learning management system) keeps grades and credit. This sits next to it and does not replace it.

We teach, or you do

Nelson teaches AI, design, photo and video. Or your own people teach in the room, the way the Lab's guest facilitators do.

Your people, your records

Every room is sign-in only, and replays stay drafts until your coordinator publishes them.
