What the workshop is

A working session with me, not a lecture.

Two hours, live and hands-on. Every attendee arrives with one repetitive task they are tired of doing, and we hand that task to AI together, step by step, in plain English. Nobody watches from the sidelines: every block ends with a checkpoint, and nobody moves on until their own result is on their own screen.

They leave with working systems, not notes. That is the whole design.

The session, block by block

Four blocks. Four checkpoints. Zero spectators.

01

Your First AI Employee

What AI is actually good at for real work: drafting, summarizing, answering, repeating. Live, a raw voice note becomes a client email, a quote, and a follow-up. Then every attendee does the same thing with their own task.

Checkpoint: everyone has one real output
02

The Content Machine

The voice-training move: feed AI three things you have already written and get ten drafts back that sound like you. Attendees generate a week of content for their own venture before the block ends.

Checkpoint: five usable posts each
03

Make It Recurring

From one-off prompts to a system that runs weekly: templates, saved instructions, and one simple automation each attendee sets up on the task they came in dreading.

Checkpoint: automation saved and tested once
04

Full Speed

I show the receipts. The campaign that filled the session, the posts, the emails, the reminders, all of it ran on the same systems they just built. Proof, not promises.

Checkpoint: they have seen it run a real business
What your students keep

They do not take notes home. They take systems.

  • One working automation, built on a task from their own week, saved and tested before they log off.

  • A week of content drafted in their own voice, from three things they already wrote.
  • Templates and saved instructions they keep and reuse long after the session ends.

Questions sponsors ask

The short answers.

Who is this for?+

Beginners. It is built for people who have never used AI past curiosity. Plain English the whole way through, and the pace is set by checkpoints, not a clock.

What do attendees need?+

A laptop, a phone, and one repetitive task they are tired of doing. That task becomes their automation by the end of the session.

Does our cohort attend in person?+

Sponsored cohorts join live online from anywhere. My public sessions run in my Dallas studio, and your block stays its own room either way.

When would our session happen?+

Around your calendar. Tell me the stretch of the semester or quarter that works for your people and we set the date there.

How is it billed?+

One invoice to your organization, billed in advance. Students never pay anything.
