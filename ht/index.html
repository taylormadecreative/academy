Meet Ada

She opens the door.
The University does the talking.

Ada is HT’s student ambassador, built for the University and licensed to it. She greets each member by name, tells the week, and introduces the students a gift reached. She never makes the ask. The President does.

How the partnership works

HT’s name on the door.
The Academy’s engine behind it.

Step one

Pick the offices

Start with the two or three spaces that have a date on the calendar: a donor weekend, a live briefing, a track that begins this term. The rest open when they are ready.

Step two

Your people, your content, our build

Each office keeps the pen. Announcements, agendas, materials, and scripts come from HT. The Academy builds, hosts, and finishes it, the way it runs today for the Atlanta University Center.

Step three

Co-branded now, HT alone when you want

It runs as Huston-Tillotson × Taylormade Academy today. When the University wants it to carry HT’s name alone, that is a switch, not a rebuild.

Where it starts

Three offices to start.

Advancement, for the donor pages and the fall briefing. Events, for the weekend on campus. Learn, for the first cohort a gift can fund. Everything else is already waiting in the hub.
