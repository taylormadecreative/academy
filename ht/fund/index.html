How it works

Three steps. One gift. No student pays.

Step one

Pick a track and a headcount

Advancement matches a donor to a track with a date on the calendar and a cohort size that fits the gift.

Step two

The gift goes to the University

HT funds the seat block. The Academy runs the seats, the live room, the materials, and the showcase. Students never see an invoice.

Step three

The donor sees who it reached

The cohort appears on the donor’s own page: the name they chose, the sessions, first names with permission, and the showcase invite.

Blocks are sized to the cohort. Advancement names the track and the headcount, and the University funds the block.

Questions donors ask

The short answers.

Can a donor name the cohort?

Yes, with the University’s approval. The name appears in the hub and on the donor’s page, and on the certificates once those ship.

Do students pay anything?

No. The gift goes to the University, the University funds the block, and students register with their HT email.

Can a company or a foundation fund one?

Yes. Same block, same page, same showcase invitation.

What does the donor receive?

Their own page with the cohort on it, a note from the President when the track begins, and a seat at the showcase.

Is this the University’s course system?

No. These tracks are co-curricular. The course system stays exactly where it is.
