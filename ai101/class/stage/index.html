Sonnet 5.5 ⌄
How it works

On its own, it doesn't look anything up. It picks the word most likely to come next, one word at a time.

Why it gets things wrong

It picks what sounds right, not what's true. So it can sound sure and still be wrong. That's a hallucination.

Need facts? Use search.

Most AI chatbots come with a search or research tool now. It pulls real information from the web and shows you its sources.

TrustGovernment sites, schools, the news, the company's own website

SkipReddit, forums and people's opinions

Open the source. Does it really say that?

Never paste:

  • Passwords
  • Social Security, bank and card numbers
  • Other people's private info
  • Customer and client info
  • Employee info
  • Company secrets

Swap real names and numbers for [brackets]. At work, follow your company's AI rules.
