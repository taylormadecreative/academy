# Taylormade Academy — Product Context

register: brand (marketing pages) / product (member-area app pages under /community/, /dashboard/, /library/, /welcome/, /course/)

## What it is
A creative learning community by Nelson Taylor (Taylormade Creative, Dallas–Fort Worth).
Members join free, hang out in an on-site community (feed, channels, DMs, member
directory), read ebooks in an on-site reader or download them, watch video courses,
and track everything from a personal dashboard. Native iOS app wraps the same site.

## The one business goal
**Sell the $15/mo Membership.** Free tier is the on-ramp (community + free Playbook
ebook); Membership unlocks every ebook + every video course. Ebooks also sell
a-la-carte ($19 / $17 / $29 bundle) as anchors that make $15/mo look obvious.
Every page should move a visitor toward: Join free → experience the community →
start the membership.

## Users
- Creatives and side-hustlers (20s–40s), many on phones, deciding if this is legit.
- Beginners: students, 9-to-5ers curious about AI + creative skills. Plain English wins.
- Nelson's existing cohort (AUC workshop alumni, clients) already inside.

## Voice
Nelson's first person. Designer-to-designer, plain English, honest about effort.
No hype, no "unleash/elevate/next-gen", no fabricated numbers (NEVER fake student
counts, ratings, or earnings). Soft closes; partnership voice, never money-hungry.

## Anti-references
- Hustle-bro course funnels (fake scarcity, income screenshots, countdown timers).
- Generic SaaS template look (rainbow feature icons, three equal card rows).
- Skool/Circle clones with no personality.
