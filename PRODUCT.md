# HT Hub — Product Context

register: product (student, staff, and leadership workspace under `/ht/hub/`)

## What it is

HT Hub is a proposed, campus-wide digital home prepared by Taylormade Academy for Huston-Tillotson University. It brings learning and campus life into one student-centered experience: courses, assignments and grades, cohort classrooms, events, community conversations, direct messages, campus resources, and support. The central promise is **everything around the classroom, in one place**.

The current project is a local, interactive demonstration with illustrative people and records. It is not an institutional deployment and does not claim university approval, production integrations, or live video service. Keep demo labels visible and identify sample activity as sample. Never imply that sample enrollments, grades, attendance, meetings, analytics, or student stories are real.

The intended direction is a native learning platform and campus hub, not a Canvas-dependent experience. Build the core teaching workflow here: course sections, learning materials, assignments, submissions, feedback, grades, cohort sessions, and student progress. Do not add Canvas integration as a shortcut or dependency.

## Users and needs

- Students need a clear next action, a reliable view of classes and deadlines, quick contact with peers and staff, and an obvious route to support.
- Instructors and campus staff need to publish learning and campus information, manage their sections, respond to students, and see work within their permission.
- Leadership needs a carefully scoped view of participation and activity, with transparent sample labels in demos and privacy-respecting aggregate reporting in a real deployment.
- Campus partners and visitors need a simple route to relevant offices, events, and opportunities.

## Product rules

- Today is the landing page: show the learner's next meaningful action and the next relevant campus or class touchpoint before secondary detail.
- Community and direct messages are core destinations, visible in the primary navigation.
- Keep the five everyday destinations easy to scan: Today, Learning, Community, Messages, and Campus. Place less frequent destinations such as Events and Classrooms under a clearly labeled More menu; keep Get help in the header.
- Courses and learning pathways are distinct records and workflows. Explain that distinction plainly.
- Ada is a campus guide. The written onboarding must work without a video; add a recorded welcome only when a real recording is configured.
- Avoid fake user counts, ratings, institutional endorsements, and claims that a sample action reached real people.
- Use plain, warm language that respects the university's community and history. Avoid generic SaaS claims such as “best LMS ever” or unsupported “AI-powered” promises.

## Brand and relationship

Show Huston-Tillotson as the campus context and Taylormade Academy as the builder/partner: “HT Hub with Taylormade Academy.” Use university marks only in the provided proposal/demo context and respect the existing ownership notice. Treat an HT-only white-label version as a future option requiring a separate brand decision.

## Demo standard

Demonstrate a believable student journey: orient in the Guide, open a cohort course, see an upcoming class, continue a learning activity, message a campus contact, join a community conversation, find an event, and reach support. Each step should work, disclose when it is illustrative, and avoid claiming a real service connection where none exists.
