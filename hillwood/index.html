How it runs

Nobody sits through a deck.

AI Foundations is a working session, 60 to 90 minutes, built around Hillwood’s approved tools and your people’s actual tasks. Five beats, in this order.

  1. 01What AI is, and isn’t

    Plain language, one diagram, no jargon. Enough to stop guessing.

  2. 02Hillwood’s tools, Hillwood’s rules

    The approved tools, and what never goes into a public one.

  3. 03Five everyday workflows

    Email, summarizing a document, meeting notes, first drafts, questions to a spreadsheet.

  4. 04Prompting that works

    One framework, practiced live on tasks people brought with them.

  5. 05Leave with it

    A one-page prompt guide, and the replay where the team can find it.
