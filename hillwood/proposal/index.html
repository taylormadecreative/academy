Three ways in

Start small, or start the program.

Every path is built from the same six formats below, so the math is plain and any piece can be swapped. I would start in the middle.

The six formats

What things cost.

A deposit holds the date and the balance is due on delivery, by direct ACH. W-9 on request. Every live session includes the Hillwood room for that session and its replay, the recording, and a one-page guide. The Hub keeps the room open all year. The produced module is quoted as a range until scope and shoot days are set.

Course by course

Every team, every level.

Thirty-six sessions across eight teams and levels, from the all-staff hour to the Champions cohort. Each one names what people can do the Monday after. Filter by team or by format; open any row for who it is for and why it fits Hillwood.

How it runs

Your room, or mine.

Nobody sits through a deck. Every session is hands-on inside the tools IT has approved, built on sample documents, or your own once IT clears them, with a replay and a guide afterward.

On Taylormade AcademyThe Hillwood room

One link, sign in with a Hillwood email, browser only, nothing to install. Cameras, screen share, chat, breakouts, spotlight. Records itself; the replay stays in the room.

Dallas or Fort WorthIn person

Turtle Creek or Alliance. Laptops open, everyone building alongside me. Same curriculum, recorded into the room if you want it.

Both officesHybrid

One office in person with me, the other in the Hillwood room, same hour. Dallas and Fort Worth without anyone driving.

For the yearThe Hillwood Hub

The room stays open: every replay, the materials library, a new-hire track, sign-in lists, and HR or the Champions can run their own sessions in it.
