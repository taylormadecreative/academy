The 6 Recipes
Recipe 01 — The 6am Morning Brief
Every weekday at 7am, read my unread Gmail from the last 24 hours and my #team and #clients Slack channels.

Sort what actually matters into three buckets: Urgent (needs me today), Reply today, and FYI. Ignore newsletters, receipts, promotions, and automated notifications.

Write it as a brief I can read in two minutes: a one-line summary at the top, then each bucket as a short list showing the sender and the one thing they need. Keep it tight.

Do not reply to anything or take any action. Just write the brief and save it.
Recipe 02 — Inbox Triage
Go through my unread email from today. Sort each one into Client, Invoice, Newsletter, or Other.

For anything that is a routine client question, draft a reply in my voice. Match the tone of the three past replies I am giving you as examples. Keep replies short and warm.

Do not send anything. Leave every reply as a draft in Gmail for me to review. At the top, give me a one-line list of what you drafted and what still needs me.

Flag anything you are unsure about instead of guessing.
Recipe 03 — The Excel Machine
Take every spreadsheet and CSV in this folder and combine them into one clean Excel workbook.

Make two tabs. A Raw Data tab with everything stacked and de-duplicated. A Summary tab with monthly totals using SUMIFS, a percent-change column versus the prior month, and a total row.

Add conditional formatting on the Summary tab: highlight any month down more than 10 percent in red, and mark the healthy months so I can scan it fast.

Name the file Q2-Summary.xlsx and save it to this folder.
Recipe 04 — Content on Autopilot
Each morning, draft one Instagram caption and one LinkedIn post in my voice, using the brand notes in this folder.

Pick a fresh angle each day from my topic list. First person, the way I actually talk. For Instagram, keep it to five hashtags at most.

Line them up for me to approve. Do not post anything.

Give me the two drafts plus a one-line note on why you picked today's angle, so I can approve in ten seconds.
Recipe 05 — The Ad-Spend Dashboard
Connect my Google Ads and Meta Ads. Build a live dashboard that shows, for each account: spend so far this month, the daily pace, and the projected month-end total against a combined budget of $6,000.

Show a progress bar for each account and one for the combined total. Flag any account pacing over budget in red, and tell me exactly how much to cut per day to land on goal.

Make it a clean HTML dashboard I can open any time, and update it daily.
Recipe 06 — The Client Research Brief
Before my call with Riverside Dental, build me a one-page brief.

Cover: what they do and how big they are, their locations, their recent reviews and overall reputation, who the likely decision-makers are, and anything notable in the last few months.

Then give me three specific angles I could open the conversation with, based on what you found. Keep the whole thing to one page I can read in the parking lot.

Cite where each fact came from so I can trust it.

Keep it running
