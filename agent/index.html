First, what is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a helper you set up once that does a task for you every time after that. You describe the job in plain English and give it what it needs. It reads, decides, and acts. No code, no engineering degree, no waiting on a developer.

Picture a salon owner's agent: it reads every new inquiry, answers the three questions everyone asks, and offers a consult time. She checks what it did over coffee.

Friday, October 23, 7 to 9 PM CT, live on the Academy player. You watch me build on screen and build the same thing on your own laptop, with a live chat for questions the whole way. The tools are free to start and I walk you through the accounts. We build in three moves.

    Pick the one task that eats your week.

  2. 2 Build

  3. 3 Show

    Everyone drops what their agent did into the live chat, and I read them out. You leave with it working, and the playbook to build the next one.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the job in plain English. The building is clicking, pasting, and testing. If you can write a text message, you can do this.

What do I need?

A laptop or desktop with a browser, and a free Claude account (recommended) or a free ChatGPT account, set up before 7 PM. Bring one task you want handled and a few real examples of it. Phones are fine for watching but not for building.

Do I have to pay for the tools?

Not on the night. Everything we build runs on free accounts. If you want to use your agent every day afterward, that may take one subscription of about $20 a month on the platform you choose. I say that now so there are no surprises later.

What does it cost?

An online seat is $75 early bird through Friday, October 16, then $90. A seat in the studio, building next to me, is $125, and there are only 15. People who come to the free AI 101 on October 9 get a lower price for 48 hours afterward, through the link in their email.

Is it online or in person?

Both. Most seats are online, live on the Taylormade Academy player. There are also 15 seats in my Dallas studio; the address comes in your ticket email. Your ticket email has the sign-in link; you sign in with a free Academy account and the room opens at 6:45 PM CT. It is one-way video with a live chat, so you can ask questions the whole way through. It is not a Zoom call, and nobody sees your camera.
